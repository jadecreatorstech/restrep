Privacy Policy — RestRep

Effective date: [set on first release]

RestRep stores your workout data only on your own device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account, no cloud sync, no advertising, and no analytics in the app.

What the app stores — on your device only
When you use RestRep, the following is kept in a private on-device database:
Your exercise library (exercise names, default weights, rest times, rep settings)
Your workout groups (routines)
Your workout history (dates, exercises performed, sets, reps, weights)
None of this ever leaves your phone or tablet.

What we do NOT do
No accounts or sign-in — we never see your name, email, or any identifier.
No analytics or crash reporting — no usage data of any kind leaves the device.
No advertising — no ad SDKs, no tracking pixels.
No cloud sync — there are no RestRep servers; your data is not uploaded anywhere.
No location, contacts, camera, or health data. The app requests only the notification permission, so rest-timer alerts can fire while the app is in the background. Notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device.
Device backups: if you back up your phone with iCloud or Google Backup, your workout data is included in that backup under Apple/Google's own backup policies — not ours. We have no access to it.

Third parties
The current version of RestRep includes no third-party SDKs that transmit data (the app is built with Flutter and stores data locally with SQLite). If a future version adds anything that changes this — for example cloud sync or crash reporting — this policy will be updated and posted before that version is released.

Children
The app is a general workout timer and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with the effective date updated above, before the corresponding app update is released.
